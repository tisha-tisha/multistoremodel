Sensory Information enters your Multi-Store Model through any of your 5 senses.

Capacity: All sensory experience

Encoding: Sensory specific (any of 5 senses)

Duration: 1/4 or 1/2 second

⬇

If you paid attention to these sensory clues and rehearsed them, it moves to Short Term Memory!

If you didn't or it wasn't rehearsed, then the information is lost.

Stage 2: Short Term Memory

Your attention causes your inner voice's thoughts on the sensory information. This means it has entered Short Term Memory.

Capacity: 5-9 items

Encoding: Acoustic (your inner dialogue!)

Duration: 15-30 seconds

⬇

If you rehearsed these thoughts in your head, it transfers to Long Term Memory!

If you didn't, it gets displaced (new information replaces it) or it decays and is lost.

Stage 3: Long Term Memory

✦ ✧ ✦ ✧ ✦ ✧ ✦

Thoughts that have entered Long Term Memory, it SHOULD stay there forever!

Capacity: Unlimited

Encoding: Semantic (meaning you associate to the thoughts)

Duration: Lifetime/ forever!

However, while information can't be completely forgotten here, it can seem lost to you when can no longer successfully access/retrieve them. This could be due to decay over time, lack of cues to jog your memory or any interference like amnesia illnesses.
